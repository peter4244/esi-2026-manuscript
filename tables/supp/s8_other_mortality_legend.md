**ST8. Other-cause mortality by MD-COPD diagnostic category under both frameworks.**

Cause-specific Cox proportional-hazards models estimate the hazard ratio for other-cause mortality relative to the noCOPD reference under each framework, with deaths from cardiovascular disease and cancer treated as censored. Adjustment covariates are age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, and body mass index. Category totals under the CT-based framework (at risk; the ESI-based categories contain different participants): AFL-only-noCOPD n = 170, COPD minor pathway n = 1,086, COPD major pathway n = 3,943.

**Abbreviations:** CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; MD-COPD, multidimensional COPD; AFL, airflow limitation; HR, hazard ratio; CI, confidence interval.
