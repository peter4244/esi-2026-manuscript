**Supplementary Table S9b. Pairwise log-IRR contrasts among cross-classification groups for exacerbations.**

Wald contrasts on the log-incidence-rate-ratio scale between each pair of the three COPD-positive cross-classification groups (Both-COPD, Bhatt-only-COPD, ESI-only-COPD) for the exacerbation-count outcome. Estimates are the difference in log IRR between the two groups in the contrast; the standard error is the Wald SE from the fitted negative-binomial model. Adjusted p-values are single-step-adjusted across the three contrasts using the multcomp package. Corresponding contrasts for the two mortality outcomes are in Supplementary Table S9a.

**Abbreviations:** IRR, incidence-rate ratio; SE, standard error; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography.
