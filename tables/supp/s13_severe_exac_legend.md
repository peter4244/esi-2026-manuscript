**ST13. Sensitivity analysis: Category-level associations for severe exacerbations only.**

This sensitivity analysis refits the primary negative-binomial exacerbation model restricted to severe exacerbations only (all-cause and respiratory mortality outcomes are unchanged). Adjustment covariates match the Table 1 exacerbation covariate set: age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, body mass index and prior exacerbation frequency.
