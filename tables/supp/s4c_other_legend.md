**Supplementary Table S4c. Other-cause (non-CVD, non-cancer) mortality by MD-COPD diagnostic category under both frameworks.**

Cause-specific Cox proportional-hazards models estimate the hazard ratio for other-cause mortality (all primary causes of death outside the CVD I00–I99 and cancer C00–D48 chapters) relative to the noCOPD reference under each framework, with deaths from CVD and cancer treated as censored. This category includes respiratory-attributable deaths (J-chapter), which are analyzed separately as the primary respiratory mortality outcome in Table 1. Adjustment covariates are age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, and body mass index. Category totals (MD-COPD, at risk): AFL-only-noCOPD n = 170, COPD-minor n = 1,099, COPD-major n = 3,969.

**Abbreviations:** CVD, cardiovascular disease; CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; AFL, airflow limitation; HR, hazard ratio; CI, confidence interval; ICD-10, International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision.
