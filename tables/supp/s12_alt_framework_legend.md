**ST12. Sensitivity analysis: Category-level associations under the alternative 4-criterion framework.**

This sensitivity analysis refits the primary Table 1 analysis under an alternative 4-criterion version of the MD-COPD framework in which only the emphysema criterion is replaced by the ESI-based rule; the airway-wall-thickening criterion is retained in its original CT-defined form. Adjustment covariates match the Table 1 covariate set: age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years and body mass index for mortality, with prior exacerbation frequency additionally included in the exacerbation models.

**Abbreviations:** CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; MD-COPD, multidimensional COPD; AFL, airflow limitation; HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; CI, confidence interval.
