**Supplementary Table S8. Paired-bootstrap test of category-level equivalence between frameworks (ΔlogHR).**

Formal test of framework equivalence at the diagnostic-category level. For each combination of mortality outcome (all-cause, respiratory) and Bhatt category (COPD-minor, COPD-major), the paired-bootstrap procedure resamples participants with replacement (n = 2,000 replicates) and refits the Cox proportional-hazards models under both frameworks; the mean between-framework difference on the log-hazard-ratio scale (ΔlogHR) and its 95% percentile interval are reported alongside the point-estimate HR under each framework. The two-sided p-value tests the null hypothesis of equal log-hazard-ratios between frameworks. Adjustment covariates within each bootstrap replicate match the Table 1 covariate set (age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, body mass index).

**Abbreviations:** HR, hazard ratio; ΔlogHR, difference in log-hazard-ratio between the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks; CI, confidence interval; CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index.
