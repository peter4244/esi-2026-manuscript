**Supplementary Table S3a. Sensitivity analysis: category-level associations after excluding participants at the ESI ceiling (ESI = 10).**

Pre-specified sensitivity analysis to verify that participants at the upper ESI ceiling did not disproportionately drive the primary category-level associations in Table 1. The Cox proportional-hazards (mortality) and negative-binomial (exacerbations) models from Table 1 are refit after excluding n = 232 ceiling-value participants; adjustment covariates and reference category (noCOPD) are unchanged. Point estimates and 95% confidence intervals remain within the same magnitude range as the primary Table 1 estimates across all three outcomes and both frameworks.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography; AFL, airflow limitation; HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; CI, confidence interval.
