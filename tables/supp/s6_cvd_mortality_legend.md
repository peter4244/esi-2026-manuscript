**ST6. Cardiovascular-disease-specific mortality by MD-COPD diagnostic category under both frameworks.**

Cause-specific Cox proportional-hazards models estimate the hazard ratio for cardiovascular-disease mortality relative to the noCOPD reference under each framework, with deaths from other causes treated as censored. Adjustment covariates are age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, and body mass index. Category totals under the CT-based framework (at risk; the ESI-based categories contain different participants): AFL-only-noCOPD n = 170, COPD, minor pathway n = 1,086, COPD, major pathway n = 3,943.

**Abbreviations:** CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; MD-COPD, multidimensional COPD; AFL, airflow limitation; HR, hazard ratio; CI, confidence interval.
