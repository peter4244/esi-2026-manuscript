**ST5. Per-stratum Pearson correlations between ESI and quantitative CT emphysema metrics.**

Pearson correlations between the Emphysema Severity Index (ESI) and two quantitative CT emphysema measures (%LAA-950HU on inspiration and PRM-defined percent emphysema) are reported within each baseline GOLD-stratum and pooled across the full analytic cohort. Per-stratum N reflects participants with non-missing values for both variables in the pair. Correlations are weak in the strata with minimal structural disease (Never, GOLD 0, PRISm) and become progressively stronger with increasing airflow obstruction (GOLD 2–4), consistent with a ceiling on the ESI dynamic range in participants without emphysema.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; LAA-950HU, percent low-attenuation lung area with attenuation below −950 Hounsfield units on inspiration; PRM, parametric response mapping; GOLD, Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease; PRISm, preserved-ratio impaired spirometry; N, number of participants with non-missing values for both variables in the pair.
