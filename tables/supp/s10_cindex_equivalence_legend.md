**ST10. Discrimination-level equivalence between the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks.**

Harrell's C-index is estimated for the Cox proportional-hazards model of each mortality outcome under both frameworks with identical adjustment covariates (age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, body mass index). Paired concordance is compared subject by subject to obtain the between-framework Δ C-index and its 95% confidence interval. Equivalence within a ±0.02 a priori margin is tested using two one-sided tests (TOST); both one-sided tests reject the null of a difference exceeding the margin, so the two frameworks are declared equivalent at the discrimination level for both all-cause and respiratory mortality.

**Abbreviations:** C-index, Harrell's concordance index; CI, confidence interval; TOST, two one-sided tests; CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index.
