**Supplementary Table S3c. Sensitivity analysis: category-level associations for severe exacerbations only.**

Pre-specified sensitivity analysis to verify that the primary exacerbation associations in Table 1 are not driven by mild or ambiguously-defined events. The negative-binomial exacerbation model is refit with the outcome restricted to severe exacerbations, defined as those requiring an emergency-department visit or hospitalization; adjustment covariates and reference category (noCOPD) are unchanged. Incidence-rate ratios for the COPD-minor and COPD-major categories remain elevated with confidence intervals overlapping the primary all-severity estimates in Table 1.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography; AFL, airflow limitation; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; CI, confidence interval.
