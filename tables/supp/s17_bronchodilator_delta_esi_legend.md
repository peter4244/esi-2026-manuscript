**ST17. ESI stability across paired pre- and post-bronchodilator spirometry.**

Change in the Emphysema Severity Index (ESI) between paired pre- and post-bronchodilator spirometry measurements at the baseline visit. Pairs are restricted to measurements with valid ESI values computed from both pre- and post-bronchodilator flow-volume curves. This analysis is not restricted to the analytic cohort and draws on all COPDGene participants with paired spirometry, so the number of pairs exceeds the analytic cohort size.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; ΔESI, post-bronchodilator minus pre-bronchodilator ESI.
