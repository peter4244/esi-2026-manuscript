**ST17. ESI stability across paired pre- and post-bronchodilator spirometry.**

Change in the Emphysema Severity Index (ESI) between paired pre- and post-bronchodilator spirometry measurements at the baseline visit. Pairs are restricted to participants with valid ESI values computed from both pre- and post-bronchodilator flow-volume curves.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; ΔESI, post-bronchodilator minus pre-bronchodilator ESI.
