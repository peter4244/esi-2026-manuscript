**Supplementary Table S9a. Pairwise log-HR contrasts among cross-classification groups for all-cause and respiratory mortality.**

Wald contrasts on the log-hazard-ratio scale between each pair of the three COPD-positive cross-classification groups (Both-COPD, Bhatt-only-COPD, ESI-only-COPD), reported separately for the two mortality outcomes. Estimates are the difference in log HR between the two groups in the contrast; the standard error is the Wald SE from the fitted Cox model. Adjusted p-values are single-step-adjusted within each outcome across the three contrasts using the multcomp package. Corresponding contrasts for the exacerbation outcome are in Supplementary Table S9b.

**Abbreviations:** HR, hazard ratio; SE, standard error; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography.
