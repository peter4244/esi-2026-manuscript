**Supplementary Table S3b. Sensitivity analysis: category-level associations under the alternative 4-criterion framework (emphysema criterion replaced).**

Pre-specified sensitivity analysis to verify that the observed diagnostic and prognostic performance of the ESI-based framework does not depend on the specific form of the MD-COPD structural component. The primary Cox proportional-hazards (mortality) and negative-binomial (exacerbations) models are refit under an alternative 4-criterion framework in which only the emphysema criterion is replaced by the ESI-based rule; the airway-wall-thickening criterion is retained in its original CT-defined form. Point estimates across all three outcomes and both frameworks remain within the same magnitude range as the primary Table 1 estimates.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography; AFL, airflow limitation; HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; CI, confidence interval.
