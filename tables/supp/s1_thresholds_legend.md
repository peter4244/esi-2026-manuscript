**Supplementary Table S1. Threshold sensitivity: candidate ESI-based COPD definitions vs the CT-based MD-COPD reference.**

Ten candidate ESI-based COPD definitions were evaluated for their ability to reproduce the CT-based MD-COPD label in the analytic cohort (n = 9,463). Definitions vary along three axes: number of minor criteria in the panel (4 vs 5), single-cutoff (4-criteria) vs two-threshold (5-criteria) scoring of ESI, and the minor-criteria count required for the "minor" designation. Sensitivity, specificity, and Cohen's κ are reported for each definition against the CT-based reference. The "Selected" column marks the training-derived variant (5-criteria, T_low = 1.0, T_high = 2.5) used in all main-manuscript analyses.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; κ, Cohen's kappa coefficient; T_low / T_high, low / high ESI thresholds used in the two-threshold 5-criteria definition.
