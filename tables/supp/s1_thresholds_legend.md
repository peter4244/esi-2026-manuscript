**ST1. Threshold sensitivity: candidate ESI-based COPD definitions vs the CT-based MD-COPD reference.**

Ten candidate ESI-based COPD definitions were evaluated for their ability to reproduce the CT-based MD-COPD label. Each candidate is reported twice: on the full analytic cohort (n = 9,402, columns prefixed "Full") and on a held-out 20% split (n = 1,880, columns prefixed "Test") not used to derive the ESI cut-points. The cut-points were obtained from a single-variable classification tree fit to the remaining 80%, so the full-cohort columns are in-sample with respect to threshold selection and the held-out columns are the independent estimate. Definitions vary along three axes: the number of minor criteria in the panel (4 vs 5), the ESI scoring rule (single-cutoff for the 4-criteria variants, two-threshold for the 5-criteria variants), and the minor-criteria count required for the "COPD-minor" designation. Sensitivity, specificity, and Cohen's κ are reported for each definition against the CT-based MD-COPD label. The training-derived variant (5 criteria, cutoff low = 1.0, cutoff high = 2.5) is the definition used in all main text analyses.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; CT, computed tomography; MD-COPD, multidimensional COPD; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; N, number of participants classified as COPD by the candidate definition within the stated population; κ, Cohen's kappa coefficient; ESI cutoff, single ESI threshold above which the emphysema criterion is considered met in a 4-criteria variant; ≥N of M minor, minimum count of minor criteria (of M total) required for the COPD-minor classification; cutoff low / cutoff high, lower / upper ESI thresholds in a 5-criteria two-threshold variant (values below the low cutoff contribute zero minor criteria; low cutoff ≤ ESI < high cutoff contribute one; ESI ≥ high cutoff contribute two).
