**ST16. ESI trajectory by baseline lung-function stratum, follow-up visits 2 and 3.**

Change in ESI (ΔESI = follow-up − baseline) is summarized as mean and median within the GOLD 0 and PRISm baseline strata at follow-up visits 2 and 3. N is the number of participants with a valid ESI value at each follow-up visit. Positive ΔESI values indicate emphysema progression; negative individual values indicate apparent regression, most commonly attributable to scanner-to-scanner variability or measurement noise; all group means and medians shown here are non-negative. Despite similar baseline ESI values, PRISm participants show larger longitudinal increases than GOLD 0 participants, consistent with a gradual accumulation of emphysema not captured by baseline FEV₁/FVC alone.

**Abbreviations:** ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; ΔESI, change in ESI from baseline; GOLD, Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease; PRISm, preserved-ratio impaired spirometry; FEV₁, forced expiratory volume in one second; FVC, forced vital capacity.
