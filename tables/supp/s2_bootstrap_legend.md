**ST2. Paired-bootstrap test of category-level equivalence between the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks, all outcomes.**

Formal test of framework equivalence at the diagnostic-category level for each combination of outcome and MD-COPD category. For each combination, the paired-bootstrap procedure resamples participants with replacement (n = 1,000 replicates) and refits the category model under both frameworks (Cox proportional-hazards for mortality; negative-binomial with follow-up-year offset for exacerbations). The mean between-framework difference on the log-effect scale (log HR for mortality, log IRR for exacerbations) and its 95% percentile interval are reported alongside the point-estimate effect size under each framework. The two-sided p-value tests the null hypothesis of equal log-effects between frameworks; values reported as "<0.002" denote outcome-category combinations in which no resample crossed zero, i.e., p below the 1/500 resolution of a two-sided percentile test. Adjustment covariates within each bootstrap replicate match the Table 1 covariate set (age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, body mass index).

The "Difference (CT − ESI)" column is the signed difference between the two point estimates on the natural scale; it is not an absolute value and may be negative. noCOPD denotes participants meeting neither framework's COPD definition.

**Abbreviations:** HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; Δlog, difference in log-effect (log HR for mortality, log IRR for exacerbations) between the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks; CI, confidence interval; CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index.
