**ST2. Paired-bootstrap test of category-level equivalence between the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks, all outcomes.**

Formal test of framework equivalence at the diagnostic-category level for each combination of outcome and MD-COPD category. For each combination, the paired-bootstrap procedure resamples participants with replacement (n = 1,000 replicates) and refits the category model under both frameworks (Cox proportional-hazards for mortality; negative-binomial with follow-up-year offset for exacerbations). Replicates in which a model failed to converge are discarded for that outcome only; 1,000 of 1,000 replicates contributed to the all-cause and exacerbation rows, but only 416 of 1,000 contributed to the respiratory rows, because respiratory deaths are sparse in the noCOPD reference group under the TORCH underlying-cause definition. The respiratory intervals and p-values should therefore be read as less stable than the all-cause and exacerbation ones, and the discarded replicates are not a random subset of all replicates. The mean between-framework difference on the log-effect scale (log HR for mortality, log IRR for exacerbations) and its 95% percentile interval are reported alongside the point-estimate effect size under each framework. The two-sided p-value tests the null hypothesis of equal log-effects between frameworks; values reported as "<0.002" denote iterations in which no resample crossed zero, i.e. p below the 1/500 resolution of a two-sided percentile test. Adjustment covariates within each bootstrap replicate match the Table 1 covariate set (age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, body mass index).

**Abbreviations:** HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; Δlog, difference in log-effect (log HR for mortality, log IRR for exacerbations) between the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks; CI, confidence interval; CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index.
