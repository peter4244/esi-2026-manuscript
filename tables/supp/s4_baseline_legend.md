**ST4. Baseline characteristics of the analytic cohort by GOLD-stage stratum.**

Continuous variables are reported as mean (standard deviation); binary and categorical variables are reported as percent. Strata are defined by the standard GOLD spirometric criteria: Never = never-smokers with normal spirometry; GOLD 0 = ever-smokers with normal spirometry; PRISm = preserved-ratio impaired spirometry (FEV₁/FVC ≥ 0.70 and FEV₁ %pred < 80); GOLD1–4 = increasing airflow limitation among participants with FEV₁/FVC < 0.70. The Overall row summarizes the full analytic cohort (n = 9,402). Column definitions: N, number of participants in the stratum; Age, age at baseline (years); Female, percent female participants; Smoker, percent current smokers at baseline; BMI, body mass index (kg/m²); Pack-yr, cumulative pack-years of cigarette exposure; FEV₁ %pred, post-bronchodilator FEV₁ as a percent of predicted; FEV₁/FVC, post-bronchodilator FEV₁/FVC ratio; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; LAA-950HU, percent low-attenuation lung area with attenuation below −950 Hounsfield units; mMRC ≥ 2, percent of participants with modified Medical Research Council dyspnea scale score ≥ 2; SGRQ ≥ 25, percent of participants with St. George's Respiratory Questionnaire total score ≥ 25; Bronchitis, percent of participants meeting the chronic bronchitis definition.

**Abbreviations:** GOLD, Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease; PRISm, preserved-ratio impaired spirometry; FEV₁, forced expiratory volume in one second; %pred, percent of predicted; FVC, forced vital capacity; BMI, body mass index; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; LAA-950HU, percent low-attenuation lung area with attenuation below −950 Hounsfield units; mMRC, modified Medical Research Council dyspnea scale; SGRQ, St. George's Respiratory Questionnaire.
