**ST15. Continuous ESI vs FEV₁/FVC as predictors of FEV₁ decline (mL/yr), stratified by baseline lung-function stratum.**

Linear mixed-effects models of longitudinal FEV₁ with a random intercept per participant are fit within each baseline stratum (GOLD 0, PRISm), in each case with continuous ESI alone and jointly with FEV₁/FVC. Estimates are reported within stratum only: pooled across the full cohort, baseline ESI is confounded by disease severity, since high ESI is concentrated among participants with severe airflow obstruction who have the least remaining lung function to lose. Slopes estimate the change in the annual FEV₁ rate per one-unit increase in ESI (or FEV₁/FVC), with the standard error in parentheses. Adjustment covariates are age at baseline, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, and baseline height, matching the anthropometric-adjustment convention used for FEV₁-decline modeling in Supplemental Table 9. Companion Supplemental Table 14 shows continuous ESI vs FEV₁/FVC as predictors of the categorical outcomes (mortality + exacerbations).

**Abbreviations:** N, number of participants in the stratum included in the model; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; FEV₁, forced expiratory volume in one second; FVC, forced vital capacity; SE, standard error; GOLD, Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease; PRISm, preserved-ratio impaired spirometry.
