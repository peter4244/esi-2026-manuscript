**Table 2. Crude rate ratios by cross-classification group.**

Each estimate is the observed event rate in the group divided by the observed rate in the Both-noCOPD reference, with 95% percentile intervals from a subject resample bootstrap in which the reference rate is recomputed in every resample. No adjustment is applied, so these are the contrasts participants in each group actually experienced. Analysis is restricted to the preserved-spirometry (minor pathway) subgroup, as in Figure 2; participants are cross-classified by whether they meet the COPD definition under each framework, yielding four mutually exclusive groups. Participants meeting the major criterion, including the AFL-only-noCOPD category, are outside this subgroup by definition and are therefore not represented here; their outcomes appear in Table 1. Rate ratios for mortality are computed on 5,289 participants and for exacerbations on 4,635. Respiratory deaths are uncommon in the discordant groups (1 death in ESI-only-COPD and 4 deaths in CT-only-COPD), so the respiratory intervals are correspondingly wide and should not be read as precise estimates. The observed rates, event counts and follow-up time behind every ratio are given in Supplemental Table S18. Table 3 reports the corresponding covariate-adjusted estimates.

**Abbreviations:** CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; RR, rate ratio; CI, confidence interval
