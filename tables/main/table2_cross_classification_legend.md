**Table 2. Mortality and exacerbation outcomes across the four cross-classification groups defined by CT-based and ESI-based COPD status.**

Participants are cross-classified by whether they meet the COPD definition under each framework, yielding four mutually exclusive groups: Both-noCOPD (reference), Bhatt-only-COPD (CT-based only), ESI-only-COPD (ESI-based only), and Both-COPD. Cox proportional-hazards models estimate the hazard ratio (HR) for all-cause and respiratory mortality; a negative-binomial model estimates the incidence-rate ratio (IRR) for exacerbations. Adjustment covariates are age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, and body mass index. Superscript letters after each estimate encode pairwise significance among the three COPD-positive subgroups within each outcome column: within an outcome, groups sharing a letter did not differ significantly (single-step-adjusted Wald contrasts, multcomp; α = 0.05); groups not sharing any letter differed significantly. Full pairwise contrast estimates are reported in Supplementary Tables S9a (mortality) and S9b (exacerbations).

**Abbreviations:** CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; COPD, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease; HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; CI, confidence interval; N, number of participants in the group; BMI, body mass index.
