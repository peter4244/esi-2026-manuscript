**Table 1. Associations of MD-COPD diagnostic categories with mortality and exacerbation outcomes under the CT-based and ESI-based frameworks.**

Crude rate ratios are the observed event rate in each category divided by the observed rate in the noCOPD reference of the same framework, with 95% percentile intervals from a paired subject resample bootstrap. Observed rates in the noCOPD reference are 1.49 deaths, 0.03 respiratory deaths and 12.38 exacerbations per 100 person-years under the CT-based framework, and 1.59, 0.04 and 13.73 respectively under the ESI-based framework. Adjusted estimates are hazard ratios from Cox proportional hazards models (mortality) and incidence-rate ratios from negative binomial models (exacerbations), each relative to the noCOPD reference; adjustment covariates are age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years and body mass index, with prior exacerbation frequency additionally included in the exacerbation models. Each p-value tests the difference between the CT-based and ESI-based estimate for that category on the log scale, by paired subject resample bootstrap; values reported as “<0.002” denote combinations in which no resample crossed zero. A crude interval whose lower bound is 0.00 marks a category in which a resample can contain no events at all, as for respiratory deaths in AFL-only-noCOPD, and should not be read as a precise estimate. Reading the two pairs of columns together shows how much of each association is accounted for by the covariates. Category sizes under the CT-based framework are noCOPD n = 4,203, AFL-only-noCOPD n = 170, COPD minor pathway n = 1,086 and COPD major pathway n = 3,943; the ESI-based categories contain different participants, and the full cross-classification is shown in Figure 1B.

**Abbreviations:** CT, computed tomography; ESI, Emphysema Severity Index; RR, rate ratio; HR, hazard ratio; IRR, incidence-rate ratio; CI, confidence interval
