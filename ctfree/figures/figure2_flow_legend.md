**Figure 2. Reclassification of MD-COPD-CT categories by the modified classifications.**

(A) Participant flow from MD-COPD-CT (center) to MD-NoCT (left) and MD-COPD-ESI (right). Column heights and ribbon widths are proportional to participant counts. Ribbons are colored by MD-COPD-CT category, with solid color where participants change category and pale color where classifications agree. (B) MD-NoCT cross-classified against MD-COPD-CT. (C) MD-COPD-ESI cross-classified against MD-COPD-CT. Rows indicate MD-COPD-CT categories and columns the categories assigned by the modified classification; diagonal cells indicate concordant classification and are shaded in proportion to participant count. Overall, MD-COPD-ESI agreed with MD-COPD-CT for 8,222 of 9,240 participants (89.0%) and MD-NoCT for 7,852 (85.0%) (McNemar P < 0.005). AFL-only = airflow limitation without other criteria; ESI = Emphysema Severity Index.
