**Figure 3. Unadjusted risk of clinical outcomes by diagnostic group under the three multidimensional classifications.**

Unadjusted rate ratios with 95% confidence intervals for all-cause mortality, respiratory mortality, and respiratory exacerbations (columns) according to diagnostic group (rows) under MD-COPD-CT, MD-NoCT, and MD-COPD-ESI. The common reference was the 3,856 participants classified as noCOPD by all three classifications. The x-axis is on a logarithmic scale and differs among columns. Rate ratios were calculated as the event rate in each group divided by that in the common reference. Estimates based on fewer than 10 events are shown without confidence intervals; corresponding adjusted estimates are shown in Figure S1 in the Online Supplement. AFL-only = airflow limitation without other criteria; ESI = Emphysema Severity Index.
