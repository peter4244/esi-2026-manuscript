**Figure 3. Unadjusted risk of clinical outcomes by diagnostic group under the three multidimensional classifications.**

Unadjusted rate ratios with 95% confidence intervals for each diagnostic group (rows) under MD-COPD, the ESI classification and the NoCT classification, for all-cause mortality, respiratory mortality and exacerbations (columns), against a common reference of the 3,856 participants assigned to noCOPD by all three classifications. The x axis is on a log scale and differs between columns. The unadjusted rate ratio is the event rate (events per 100 person-years) in the group divided by that in the reference. A point drawn without an interval had fewer than 10 events in that group, so no interval was estimated. Adjusted hazard ratios and incidence rate ratios are shown in Supplemental Figure S1. AFL-only = airflow limitation without other criteria.
