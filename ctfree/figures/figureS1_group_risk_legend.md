**Supplemental Figure S1. Unadjusted and adjusted risk of clinical outcomes by diagnostic group under the three multidimensional classifications.**

Unadjusted rate ratios (open circles) and adjusted estimates (filled circles), with 95% confidence intervals, are shown for each diagnostic group (rows) under MD-COPD-CT, MD-NoCT, and MD-COPD-ESI for all-cause mortality, respiratory mortality, and respiratory exacerbations (columns), relative to a common reference comprising the 3,856 participants classified as noCOPD by all three classifications (3,625 in mortality analyses and 3,411 in exacerbation analyses). The x-axis is on a logarithmic scale and differs among columns. Adjusted estimates are hazard ratios for mortality and incidence rate ratios for exacerbations from models adjusted for age, sex, race, current smoking status, pack-years, and body mass index; exacerbation models were additionally adjusted for baseline exacerbation frequency. Estimates based on fewer than 10 events are shown without confidence intervals. AFL-only = airflow limitation without other criteria; HR = hazard ratio; IRR = incidence rate ratio; ESI = Emphysema Severity Index.
