**Figure 1. Relationship of ESI to the visual CT criteria of MD-COPD.**

Mean ESI according to visual emphysema severity (A) and visual airway wall thickening category (B). Dashed vertical lines indicate the thresholds defining the corresponding CT criteria in MD-COPD: mild or greater emphysema and definite airway wall thickening. Insets show the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) for discrimination of each visual CT criterion by ESI in the overall cohort and separately among participants with airflow limitation and preserved spirometry. Numbers adjacent to the points indicate the number of participants in each CT category; 9,236 participants had a visual score for each criterion. Panels keep separate y axes because the two criteria span different ESI ranges. ESI = Emphysema Severity Index; AUC = area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.
