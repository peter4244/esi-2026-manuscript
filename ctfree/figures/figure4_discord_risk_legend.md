**Figure 4. Clinical outcomes according to concordance and discordance between MD-COPD-CT and MD-COPD-ESI classifications.**

Unadjusted rate ratios for all-cause mortality and exacerbations, with 95% confidence intervals, for the three groups either classification calls COPD, within each stratum of airflow limitation. The unadjusted rate ratio is the event rate (events per 100 person-years) in the group divided by that in the stratum reference. Reference groups are the participants classified as noCOPD by both methods among those without airflow limitation (top row) and participants classified as AFL-only by both methods among those with airflow limitation (bottom row). Intervals are exact Poisson intervals for deaths and participant-level bootstrap intervals for exacerbations. CT-only = classified as COPD by MD-COPD-CT only; ESI-only = classified as COPD by MD-COPD-ESI only; Both-COPD = classified as COPD by both; AFL-only = airflow limitation without other criteria. Respiratory mortality is not shown because the numbers of respiratory deaths are too small for estimation.
