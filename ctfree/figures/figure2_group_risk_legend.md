**Figure 2. Crude risk of each diagnostic group under the three multidimensional classifications.**

Crude rate ratios with 95% confidence intervals for each diagnostic group (rows) under MD-COPD, the ESI classification and the NoCT classification, for all-cause mortality, respiratory mortality and exacerbations (columns), against a common reference of the 3,745 participants assigned to noCOPD by all three classifications. The x axis is on a log scale and differs between columns. The crude rate ratio is the event rate in the group divided by the event rate in the reference. A point drawn without an interval had fewer than 10 events in that group, so no interval was estimated. Adjusted estimates are shown in Supplemental Figure S1. AFL-only, airflow limitation without other criteria.
