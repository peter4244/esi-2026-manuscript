**Supplemental Figure S2. Clinical, physiological, and CT characteristics according to concordance and discordance between MD-COPD-CT and MD-COPD-ESI classifications.**

Characteristics are shown for participants classified as COPD by both approaches (Both-COPD), by MD-COPD-CT only (CT-only), or by MD-COPD-ESI only (ESI-only), separately among participants with and without airflow limitation. Bars show group means with standard errors for ESI and %LAA-950HU and the percentage of participants meeting each criterion for visual emphysema, definite airway wall thickening, mMRC ≥2, SGRQ ≥25, and chronic bronchitis. Comparisons between CT-only and ESI-only used Wilcoxon rank-sum tests for ESI and %LAA-950HU and Fisher's exact tests for categorical variables. * P < 0.05; ** P < 0.01; *** P < 0.001; ns = not significant. Comparisons fixed by the classification definitions are shown without statistical testing. Group sizes without airflow limitation were Both-COPD, n = 714; CT-only, n = 85; and ESI-only, n = 501; with airflow limitation, Both-COPD, n = 3,459; CT-only, n = 350; and ESI-only, n = 82. %LAA-950HU = percentage of lung below −950 Hounsfield units at inspiration; mMRC = modified Medical Research Council dyspnea scale; SGRQ = St. George's Respiratory Questionnaire; ESI = Emphysema Severity Index.
