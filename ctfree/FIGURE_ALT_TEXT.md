## figure1_esi_ct
Two dot plots of mean Emphysema Severity Index (ESI) by visual CT category. Panel A, visual emphysema: mean ESI is close to 1 in the none (n = 3,086) and trace (n = 1,686) categories and rises through mild, moderate and confluent emphysema to 6.6 in advanced destructive emphysema (n = 306). A dashed line between trace and mild marks the MD-COPD-CT threshold. An inset lists areas under the curve of 0.78 overall, 0.75 with airflow limitation, and 0.56 without. Panel B, visual airway wall thickening: mean ESI is close to 1 when thickening is absent (n = 1,006) or borderline (n = 4,553) and 3.3 when definite (n = 3,677). A dashed line between borderline and definite marks the MD-COPD-CT threshold. An inset lists areas under the curve of 0.80 overall, 0.75 with airflow limitation, and 0.61 without.

## figure2_flow
Panel A is an alluvial diagram with three columns, MD-NoCT on the left, MD-COPD-CT in the center and MD-COPD-ESI on the right, each divided into noCOPD (gray), AFL-only (purple), COPD-minor (yellow) and COPD-major (red). Ribbons show participants moving between categories. Panels B and C are four-by-four tables of MD-COPD-CT categories (rows) against MD-NoCT categories (panel B) and MD-COPD-ESI categories (panel C), with concordant counts shaded on the diagonal. Of the participants classified as COPD-major by MD-COPD-CT, 833 are classified as AFL-only by MD-NoCT and 350 by MD-COPD-ESI.

## figure3_group_risk
Nine-panel forest plot of unadjusted rate ratios with 95% confidence intervals on a logarithmic scale, relative to the common noCOPD reference. Columns are all-cause mortality, respiratory mortality and exacerbations; rows are the AFL-only, COPD-minor and COPD-major groups. Each panel shows MD-COPD-CT, MD-COPD-ESI and MD-NoCT. In the AFL-only row, the MD-NoCT estimate lies to the right of the MD-COPD-CT and MD-COPD-ESI estimates for all three outcomes. COPD-major has the highest rate ratios under all three classifications.

## figure4_discord_risk
Four-panel forest plot of unadjusted rate ratios with 95% confidence intervals on a logarithmic scale, for all-cause mortality (left) and exacerbations (right), among participants without airflow limitation (top) and with airflow limitation (bottom). Each panel shows the CT-only, ESI-only and Both-COPD groups against the reference group of their stratum. Without airflow limitation, all three groups have rate ratios above 1 for both outcomes. With airflow limitation, the Both-COPD group has rate ratios above 3, and the confidence intervals of the CT-only and ESI-only groups include 1.

## figureS1_group_risk
Nine-panel forest plot laid out as Figure 3, showing for each diagnostic group and outcome both the unadjusted rate ratio (open circle) and the adjusted hazard ratio or incidence rate ratio (filled circle), with 95% confidence intervals on a logarithmic scale, under MD-COPD-CT, MD-COPD-ESI and MD-NoCT, relative to the common noCOPD reference.

## figureS2_profile
Bar charts comparing the Both-COPD, CT-only and ESI-only groups on seven characteristics: mean ESI, mean %LAA-950HU, and the percentage of participants with visual emphysema, definite airway wall thickening, mMRC ≥2, SGRQ ≥25 and chronic bronchitis. The top row shows participants without airflow limitation and the bottom row participants with airflow limitation. Brackets with significance marks compare CT-only with ESI-only. Without airflow limitation, nearly all CT-only participants have visual emphysema and definite wall thickening and nearly no ESI-only participants do, whereas mMRC ≥2 and SGRQ ≥25 are more frequent in ESI-only participants and chronic bronchitis is more frequent in CT-only participants.
