# COI disclosures and funding from published papers — ESI manuscript

Compiled 2026-10-08 from PubMed, PMC full-text XML (NCBI E-utilities) and NIH RePORTER.
Every statement below is quoted from a printed publication or a RePORTER record cited next to it.
Journal websites for Elsevier, Sage, Taylor & Francis, APS, JACC and OUP returned 403 or paywalled
text, so papers that are not in PMC could not be read. Each section names those papers.

Not researched here (already in hand): Hersh (ICMJE form 2026-08-30), Silverman and Bhatt
(author statements, August 2026). I found no printed statement for them dated after those
dates. The newest I saw were ERJ Open Res, June 2026 (Bhatt, Silverman) and Respir Res, March
2026 (Hersh), both earlier.
